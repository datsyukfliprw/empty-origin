# Editorial direction — provisional, not canon

## Book promise

A nineteen-year-old raid leader arrives in a world where people can inspect her level before learning her name. Learning to survive as a Warder, she must rescue a stranded man, build relationships she chooses, and make a life that belongs to her. Her initial search for home begins the story; her chosen future here carries the series.

**Binding author direction: a longer ongoing series; Maya never returns to Earth. This world is her new life.** The [series bible](../SERIES_BIBLE.md) and [Book One completion plan](BOOK_ONE_PLAN.md) now govern future development alongside these notes. Their proposed events are not established canon. No eventual homecoming, temporary Earth visit, or repeated near-return plot.

The voice remains first-person present, profane, observant, funny under pressure, and emotionally tethered to an ordinary family on Earth. Maya's retail and gaming experience are useful skills, not proof that everyone native to the world is stupid. Other people retain expertise, motives, livelihoods, and competence.

## Flexible middle and ending

Maya has chosen to follow the Earth evidence through a limited hire at Rook House. Preserve a credible means to leave Rook, and let what she discovers change the risks of staying. Greyward relationships should continue to matter after her departure. Freedom to leave an employer is distinct from a return to Earth. Her earlier longing for her parents remains real while her forward objective becomes rescuing Iven and building a future here.

Develop the chosen Warder run through practical exposure and decisions with costs. Do not award a rare class solely because Maya comes from Earth. Her emerging strengths are attention under pressure, coordination, bargaining, and deliberate use of limited resources. A combat-capable direction can grow from these without turning her instantly into Nyx.

Use gains often enough that the LitRPG promise stays active. Show what changed, how she earned or discovered it, what it costs, and what it cannot do. Alternate training, investigation, dangerous application, and consequences. Avoid serial errands that exist only to increase a counter.

By the middle, the Earth investigation and the institution controlling access to it should create a concrete conflict she can no longer solve by remaining useful and polite. Romance should affect decisions and expose competing wants, rather than occurring in isolated interludes.

The final movement should pay off an earned combination of abilities and trusted allies in Iven's rescue and a specific confrontation over the gate. Resolve the immediate antagonist, show the family reunion, make the drainage remedy work, and give Maya a durable, chosen position in the world. The proposed English-lit room is a receiving station here containing imported Earth fittings; establish that answer with physical evidence, not a last-minute dismissal of earlier clues. Maya never returns to Earth.

Give the romance an emotionally satisfying chosen commitment by the ending. If later marketed as genre romance, its relationship ending and centrality must actually fulfill that promise. The current working position is adult LitRPG adventure with substantial romance; its final relationship structure remains open. A continuing gate mystery cannot substitute for either an adventure payoff or an emotional one.

End with a specific undertaking Maya wants in this world and enough aftermath to feel what she has gained. The series grows through her company, new places, local gate mysteries, combat, relationships, and the home she builds. Answer major mysteries on schedule rather than preserving every question for an indefinitely postponed final volume.

## Romance direction and on-page intimacy

### Maya and Tolliver: binding romantic direction

“She is powerful and capable. He knows it. He can overpower her anyway. She chooses to let him. And his obsession is making her lose control, not making her small.”

Develop toward this through earned strength, specific admiration, credible physical dominance, and Maya's active invitation to surrender. Her independence and authority must survive the encounter. Tolliver should want her more as she becomes stronger; make his desire to undo her composure palpable through focused attention, reactions, and behavior. His ability to overwhelm her needs an on-page basis beyond her low level or dependence. Do not sabotage her competence, use jealousy as a substitute for obsession, or turn his old information control into romantic proof. His accountability and her freedom to refuse remain part of earning this dynamic.

The existing chapters establish attraction, boundaries, and damaged trust; this full dynamic remains to be dramatized. Use it to guide targeted revision and future scenes, without recording proposed encounters as canon. Sella remains a significant partner with her own aims; the final relationship structure is still open.

## Craft checks

- Keep the ratio of action, dialogue, reflection, and description responsive to the scene. Avoid copying the opening's rhetorical habits mechanically: repeated 'That is the thing,' numbered observations, and aphoristic paragraph endings lose force when used everywhere.
- Let conversations change information and choices. Tolliver cannot remain an endless dispenser of partial answers without a cost to his relationship with Maya.
- Preserve uncertainty. Maya's interpretation, a character's account, a System display, and independently corroborated facts are different kinds of evidence.
