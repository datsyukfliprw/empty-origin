Recorded September 24, 2026. **Decision implemented: the author requested the

## Recommendation

**Use 5.5 × 8.5 inches for the paperback**, provided a full proof confirms that the chapter openings retain their intended impact. This size gives the 96,693-word novel a comfortable, narrower reading column and a substantial but manageable book. **6 × 9 inches is the strong fallback** if preserving the approved opener proportions, lower print cost, or a consistent trim for much longer sequels takes priority.

The previous completed print interior was **6 × 9 inches and 332 physical pages**. It uses EB Garamond 11.35 pt on 13.55 pt leading, mirrored 0.76-inch inside and 0.62-inch outside margins, and large aperture-ring chapter openings. Its retained [PDF](EMPTY_ORIGIN_6x9_PRINT_INTERIOR.pdf) and archived [validation report](EMPTY_ORIGIN_6x9_PRINT_VALIDATION.json) document that version.

| Trim | Estimated physical pages | Assessment |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| **5.5 × 8.5 in** | **390–410** | Best overall balance of handling, reading column, and space for restrained System typography. Recompose the large opener stack. |
| **6 × 9 in** | **332 actual** | Spacious, validated layout; best for the current artwork and lowest page-based print cost. Broader in the hand. |
| **5.25 × 8 in** | **440–470** | Compact, but substantially thicker, with more pressure on chapter openings and page turns. No stronger fit here. |
| **5 × 8 in** | **470–500** | More compact still, with little room for this design at the existing type size. |

The smaller-trim page counts are **rough area-based estimates, not pagination from finished layouts**. They assume comparable typography and margins. Chapter starts, protected System/reaction groups, intentional page turns, and final front/back matter can shift the totals. The 6 × 9 count belongs to the September 20 frozen 96,693-word master. If the manuscript changes, recalculate every option from the new approved text.

At the existing margins, the text column is 4.62 inches wide at 6 × 9 and would be about 4.12 inches at 5.5 × 8.5. The narrower column suits the close first-person prose and dialogue. System notifications are text, not wide tables, so they do not alone call for a 6-inch page. Keep the body type comfortably readable; adapt the 190 pt aperture, tracked chapter heading, metadata spacing, and opening sink for the smaller page. The author-approved reference image remains the visual authority for the ring and opener character.

## Cost and production context

Under KDP's Amazon.com black-ink cream/white-paper paperback rate checked September 24, 2026, regular trims above 110 pages cost **$1.00 + $0.012 per page**. That is about **$4.98** at the actual 332 pages and **$5.80** at an illustrative 400 pages: roughly **$0.82 more per copy** for the smaller-trim example. Both 5.5 × 8.5 and 6 × 9 are regular trims and are offered for black-ink paperback and hardcover by KDP. Confirm current rates and specifications when publishing.
