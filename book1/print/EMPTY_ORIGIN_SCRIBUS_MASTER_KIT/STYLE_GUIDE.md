# Empty Origin — Scribus Production Style Guide

## Page
- Trim: **6 × 9 in**
- White/no-fill interior; cream paper supplies the tone
- Facing pages: yes
- No bleed for text pages
- Inside 0.76 in / 54.72 pt
- Outside 0.62 in / 44.64 pt
- Top 0.54 in / 38.88 pt
- Bottom 0.62 in / 44.64 pt
- Page number centered 0.39 in from bottom
- No running heads

## Fonts
- Body/display: **EB Garamond**
- System/metadata/chat: **Noto Sans Condensed**
- Numeral/page number: **Noto Serif**

## Styles
### EO Body
EB Garamond Regular, 11.35 pt, 13.55 pt fixed leading, justified, 14.5 pt first-line indent, no paragraph spacing, restrained hyphenation.

### EO Body First
Same as body, no first-line indent.

### EO Continuation
Same as body, no first-line indent. Only for deliberate paragraph continuation after a page turn.

### EO System
Noto Sans Condensed Regular, 9.15 pt, 10.9 pt leading, centered, quiet near-black, no boxes.

### EO Chat
Noto Sans Condensed Regular, 9.35 pt, 11.2 pt leading, left aligned, 18 pt left indent.

### EO Label
Noto Sans Condensed Medium, 9.6 pt, 11.5 pt leading, left aligned, 14.5 pt left indent.

### EO Chapter Title
EB Garamond, 15 pt, centered. Visual text: `C H A P T E R   O N E`.

### EO Metadata
Noto Sans Condensed Regular, 9.15 pt, centered:
`[ LOCATION: GREYWARD ]`
`[ RUN: WARDER ]`

### EO Page Number
Noto Serif Regular, 9.6 pt, centered.

## Chapter opener
The approved sample PDF in the parent print folder is the visual authority.
- Ring approx. **1.78 × 1.78 in**
- Numeral approx. 29.5–30 pt
- Chapter title immediately below ring
- LOCATION / RUN below title
- generous but economical opening sink
- Chapter One drop cap: small opening quote, D approx. 31 pt
