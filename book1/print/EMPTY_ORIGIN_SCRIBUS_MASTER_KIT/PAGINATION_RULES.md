# Empty Origin — Pagination Rules

1. Never split a System/status block.
2. Keep a System reveal with its immediate reaction whenever practical.
3. Never strand a scene-break ornament at the bottom of a page.
4. Scene break + first paragraph stay together.
5. Never leave half a sentence across a page turn.
6. Prefer a little white space over wrecking a dramatic beat.
7. Strong page turns are encouraged when intentional.
8. Chapter 1 Vharos beat: page 1 ends on `HE’S DOWN! HE’S FUCKING DOWN!`; page 2 begins the celebration/death/System confirmation.
9. Portal beat: `My eyes open.` ends the Earth-side page; alien sky starts the next.
10. Preserve chapter-ending beats such as `I go in.`.
11. Do not overuse keep-together rules.
12. Chat clusters should hang together where practical.
13. Chapter-opening stack stays together.
