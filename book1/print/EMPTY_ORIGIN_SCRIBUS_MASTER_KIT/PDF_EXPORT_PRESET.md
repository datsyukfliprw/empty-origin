# Scribus PDF Export — Print Interior Checklist

- 6 × 9 in single pages
- no bleed unless content reaches trim
- embed all fonts
- grayscale / black-and-white interior
- no cream background tint
- no crop marks for KDP
- run Scribus Preflight Verifier
- check overflow, fonts, images, empty frames, and accidental RGB fills
- re-open the final PDF and inspect System reveals and scene transitions
