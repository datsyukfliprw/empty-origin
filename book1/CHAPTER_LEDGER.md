Live continuity record for the September 19 cold-review revision through Chapter Nineteen. Earlier reviews and archived drafts are historical. Chapters remain the authority. Updated sections distinguish observed events from testimony and proposals; no current relationship endgame or return to Earth is implied.

## Chapter 01

**Opening state:**
- Maya Szczepaniak, nineteen, lives alone in Kansas City, attends community college, works at Brewed Awakening, and streams as NyxLive to a small audience.

**Major movement:**
- Maya times a gap in the boss's fire and calls the raid's movement, asking Liam to stay back until she can return to healing range. Twelve-player raid defeats Vharos the Undying in 06:42; eleven viewers watch. Maya rolls 98 for Vharos's Emberglass Saber and equips it on Nyx.
- An unexplained physical crisis incapacitates her. She subsequently wakes in an unfamiliar rainy clearing and enters the forest to escape the approaching lightning.

**Character / relationship changes:**
- Liam knows and uses her real name during the emergency. Her parents are affectionate and had offered her a return home.
- Maya's pride in independence gives way to panic and longing for her father. Their actual response on Earth is unknown.

**System / progression / resources:**
- Nyx is her silver-haired elf spellblade avatar, not Maya's transported body.
- Maya arrives human, barefoot, in black sleep shorts and an oversized CRIT HAPPENS shirt. No phone, gear, or weapon arrives with her.

**Reveals and established facts:**
- Full name: Maya Szczepaniak; pronounced Sheh-PAN-yak. She remains recognizably herself, including a small scar beside her left thumb.

**Open threads / setups / uncertainties:**
- Cause of the collapse/transition; fate of Earth body, apartment, stream, friends, and family; possibility of return.

**Ending state:**
- Alone, frightened, wet, unequipped, entering the strange forest during a storm.
