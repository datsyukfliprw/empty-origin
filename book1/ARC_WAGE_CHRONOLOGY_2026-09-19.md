## Accommodation and property

The Chapter 32 room/storage transaction is **28 bits in cash after credit for the unused return fare**. The manuscript specifies that this is the net payment. It does not invent a separate numeric fare value or gross room price. Three pieces and four bits is the actual cash paid.

Four bits later pay for the chest's replacement materials. The room and storage protections do not create a service obligation, change Maya's blank origin, or surrender her property. Her previous paid and retained lodging is not retroactively charged again.
