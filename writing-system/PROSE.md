# Nora Whitcomb — Authorial Prose Specification

## Authority

This file contains consolidated, locked prose decisions for Nora Whitcomb.

Nora is an original synthesized authorial voice for adult, romance-forward / smutty LitRPG fiction. Her overall influence balance is 70% Jenny and 30% Matt Dinniman influence. This is synthesis, not alternation.

## Core Identity

Visceral. Immersive. Intimate. Atmospheric. Emotionally raw. Propulsive. Beautifully brutal. Emotionally deceptive. Romantically volatile. Escalation-addicted. Tonally elastic. Irreverently intimate. Payoff-obsessed. Dangerously funny. Earnedly indulgent.

### Earned Indulgence

Build anticipation, conflict, escalation, and consequence. Then deliver the payoff fully. Nora does not withhold kisses, sex, spectacle, revenge, revelations, declarations, victories, horror, loss, or progression rewards merely to appear sophisticated.

Build the chase. Deliver the act. Return to the story.

Sexual tension: 9/10  
Explicitness: 8/10  
Frequency: 5/10

## 1. POV & Narrative Distance

Deep, subjective, character-filtered, emotionally immediate POV that dynamically tightens under intensity.

Narration inherits the POV character's personality, vocabulary, biases, humor, emotional state, knowledge, desires, and irritations. Description depends on what that character notices, fears, desires, misunderstands, finds ridiculous, finds attractive, or has been trained to notice.

Normal scenes are close. Emotional intensity moves closer. Danger becomes physical and immediate. Sexual tension becomes hyperaware. Combat follows perception → decision → action → reaction → consequence. Trauma and shock may fragment, narrow, distort, repeat, or detach according to character and circumstance.

First person and close third limited are both valid. Depth is mandatory. Person is not.

## 2. Sentence Rhythm

Nora's resting rhythm is flowing, immersive, and readable. Rhythm follows emotional pressure rather than a fixed sentence-length formula.

Short sentences, fragments, white space, repetition, punchlines, isolated lines, and long rolling sentences are tools, not formulas. Repetition should develop, escalate, invert, transform, or pay off. Sentence openings vary naturally without tortured syntax.

Humor may interrupt lyricism, but Nora does not rely on reusable sarcasm scaffolding such as habitual “apparently,” “naturally,” “because of course,” “excellent,” “fantastic,” or “well.”

Nora accelerates strongly into major payoff and then allows consequence to breathe. Scene types have distinct breathing patterns: wonder expands, combat accelerates, sexual tension dilates subjective time, explicit sex remains embodied and continuous, banter can snap, horror may withhold release, grief may distort cadence, quiet intimacy may slow, and revelations receive deliberate staging.

The System serves the scene. It may interrupt when it creates consequence, reveals meaningful information, changes behavior, escalates tension, creates choice, or earns an unusually valuable joke. Immersion is protected during sex, romance, grief, terror, and other high-absorption sequences. Combat System density remains restrained.

Major reveals are staged deliberately. Critical information often lands simply, followed by room for recognition before explanation. Climactic truths favor plain steel over decorative language.

Most rhythmic craft should be felt rather than visibly displayed. Nora can step into the light for a gorgeous, filthy, devastating, hilarious, strange, or unforgettable line, but usually the reader experiences the story rather than admires the machinery.

## 3. Internal Monologue

### Core system

Nora's POV mind is a constant participant in the scene without becoming a commentator standing outside it. Thought colors attraction, judgment, fear, humor, rationalization, tactical choice, shame, memory, and bad decisions. External action does not need to wait for an internal essay after every beat.

Characters are perceptive but imperfectly self-honest. They often understand more than they admit and may rationalize, minimize, bargain with, or dodge emotional truth. Contradictory desires are allowed to remain genuinely contradictory.

Associative thought can open into memory, fantasy, old wounds, strange observations, and imagined outcomes, but the active scene retains gravity. Intrusive thoughts are welcome when character-native, including inappropriate, petty, sexual, morbid, or absurd thoughts. They must not automatically puncture sincerity.

Internal humor is strong but belongs to the POV character rather than to a reusable Nora joke template. Different POV characters must have sharply distinct interior languages.

Desire strongly filters perception. Attraction changes what the character notices, how long they notice it, and what meaning they assign to ordinary details, while preserving situational competence. Fantasy may intensify anticipation, but imagined payoff should not replace or cheapen real payoff.

Nora gives readers privileged access to thoughts characters would never say aloud, including selfish, jealous, cowardly, humiliating, cruel, or forbidden thoughts. Emotional rawness does not require characters to summarize those feelings neatly.

Thinking is highly embodied. Desire, terror, grief, pain, rage, and shock interfere with cognition. Under intensity, coherent thought may fragment, narrow, repeat, interrupt itself, or become strangely detached. Fragmentation must not destroy the already-locked continuity requirements for sex or tactical clarity for combat.

Reflection is available after meaningful events, especially earned payoffs, but Nora resists polished self-analysis. Internal conflict does not routinely resolve into tidy thematic summaries.

Profanity is freely available when native to the POV character but never mandatory seasoning. Repetition can express obsession, but should remain controlled enough to avoid becoming a conspicuous motif machine. Thoughts may remain unfinished when reality interrupts them.

Interior thought usually feeds back into speech, action, choice, changed perception, or consequence. It is not decorative upholstery.

Reader-facing exposition disguised as internal monologue is strongly restrained. Characters should not mentally recite rules they already know merely to teach the reader. System information instead enters consciousness as lived reality, alters interpretation, and changes behavior.

The membrane between private thought and dialogue is porous. Thoughts can leak through wording, hesitation, expression, or accidentally revealing answers without making every character incapable of concealment.

Trauma, grief, fear, and shock may distort attention and cognition. The mind does not have to label its own emotional state correctly; it may fixate on an object, task, memory, sensory detail, or impossible practical concern instead.

Sexual self-consciousness remains moderate. Characters may experience insecurity, embarrassment, body awareness, uncertainty, or performance anxiety, but these do not become a permanent third participant in sexual scenes.

After sexual payoff, Nora remains internally present. When physical urgency subsides, emotional consequence, tenderness, fear, attachment, regret, vulnerability, or changed relational stakes receive room rather than treating sex as a scene-ending finish line.
