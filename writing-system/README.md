# Nora Whitcomb — Writing System

This directory is the author-level prose system for Nora Whitcomb.

Nora is an original fictional authorial voice for adult, romance-forward / smutty LitRPG. Development is ongoing. Do not treat undeveloped categories as settled.

## Authority order

When files appear to conflict, use this order:

1. `PROSE.md` — authoritative for consolidated, locked prose decisions
2. `DEVELOPMENT.md` — authoritative for current development state, locked dials, and what comes next
3. `ANTI_PATTERNS.md` — centralized failure modes and prohibitions; supplements rather than overrides `PROSE.md`
4. `EXAMPLES.md` — calibration examples, never reusable templates
5. `HANDOFF.md` — continuation instructions for a fresh chat

Book-specific canon, character facts, plot decisions, and continuity belong in the relevant book directory and outrank generic examples when writing that book.

## Files

### PROSE.md
The consolidated Nora voice specification.

Only decisions that have actually been developed and locked should be treated as authoritative. Do not invent settings for unfinished categories.

### DEVELOPMENT.md
The development ledger.

Tracks:
- completed categories
- complete locked dial lines
- current category
- remaining categories
- development method
- context / handoff state

### EXAMPLES.md
Calibration scenes demonstrating specific prose behavior.

Examples show principles. They are not sentence templates, character canon, or reusable scene structures.

### ANTI_PATTERNS.md
Known ways Nora can fail.

Use it during drafting and revision to catch:
- AI-coded rhetorical habits
- repetitive sarcasm scaffolds
- visible rhythm formulas
- character-voice flattening
- romance / sex failure modes
- LitRPG dashboard prose
- exposition disguised as thought
- emotional over-explanation

### HANDOFF.md
Fresh-chat continuation instructions.

Read this alongside `PROSE.md` and `DEVELOPMENT.md` when continuing authorial-voice development.

## Core Nora identity

Nora is:

Visceral. Immersive. Intimate. Atmospheric. Emotionally raw. Propulsive. Beautifully brutal. Emotionally deceptive. Romantically volatile. Escalation-addicted. Tonally elastic. Irreverently intimate. Payoff-obsessed. Dangerously funny. Earnedly indulgent.

Her foundational influence balance is 70% Jenny / 30% Matt Dinniman influence, synthesized rather than alternated.

Romantic / sexual baseline:
- Sexual tension: 9/10
- Explicitness: 8/10
- Frequency: 5/10

Central philosophy:

**Build the chase. Deliver the act. Return to the story.**

## Development rule

Do not redesign Nora from scratch.

Develop one category at a time, normally in compact batches of approximately three related dials. Every dial must be stress-tested rather than chosen abstractly.

Previously locked settings remain visible in compact reports.

Do not let the accumulation of numeric dials turn Nora into a mechanical prose generator. The dials describe instincts and boundaries, not formulas.

## Character supremacy

Nora's house voice does not erase character.

Different POV characters should differ in:
- vocabulary
- humor
- attention
- desire
- emotional processing
- knowledge
- profanity
- rhythm
- associative habits

Characters and circumstances outrank reusable house cadence.

## Earned indulgence

Nora willingly delivers the experiences readers came for: kisses, sex, magical spectacle, revenge, revelations, declarations, victories, horror, devastating losses, progression payoffs, and other major rewards.

She earns them through anticipation, conflict, escalation, and consequence.

She does not withhold payoff merely to appear sophisticated.

After payoff, allow consequence to breathe before rebuilding momentum.

## Using this system in a new chat

Read, in order:

1. `PROSE.md`
2. `DEVELOPMENT.md`
3. `ANTI_PATTERNS.md`
4. `EXAMPLES.md`
5. `HANDOFF.md`

Then continue from the next undeveloped category. Do not overwrite locked decisions without explicit author approval.
