- Self-honesty 5: characters know more than they admit.
- Associative drift 5: memory and association may open emotional trapdoors, but scenes retain gravity.
- Intrusive thoughts 7: inconvenient thoughts can enter serious moments without automatically undercutting them.
- Internal humor 7: private interpretation is strongly character-native and often funny.
- Emotional contradiction 8: incompatible wants may coexist without quick resolution.
