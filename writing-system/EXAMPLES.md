# Nora Whitcomb — Development Examples

These are calibration examples, not reusable prose templates. Do not imitate their surface constructions mechanically.

## Internal Monologue — Embodied Desire

> Riven steps closer.
>
> I know exactly what he's doing.
>
> Intimidation. Deliberate invasion of space. Make me retreat and he controls the conversation.
>
> Easy.
>
> Don't retreat.
>
> Then his fingers brush my hip.
>
> My stomach drops.
>
> There goes the fucking strategy.

Purpose: body-thought integration, desire filtering, and thought-to-action pressure.

## Internal Monologue — Shame / Forbidden Thought

> Talia survives.
>
> Everyone starts cheering.
>
> I do too.
>
> Then Riven reaches her first.
>
> He wraps both arms around her.
>
> Relief hits me.
>
> Followed immediately by something small and poisonous.
>
> I wanted him to reach for me.
>
> She's half-dead, and I'm jealous.
>
> Christ.
>
> I hug her anyway.

Purpose: expose morally uncomfortable private thought without requiring a tidy emotional lesson.

## Internal Monologue — Trauma Distortion

> The healer says Talia is dead.
>
> I hear him.
>
> I understand every word.
>
> There's blood on my boot.
>
> That's the problem.
>
> Talia hated blood on her boots.
>
> She'd polish them after every dungeon because apparently monsters could disembowel you, but looking untidy afterward was unacceptable.
>
> I should clean it before she sees.
>
> Someone touches my shoulder.
>
> “Don't.”
>
> My voice doesn't sound like mine.
>
> The blood is drying.

Purpose: demonstrate cognition distorted by grief without explicit emotional labeling.

## Internal Monologue — Post-Sex Exposure

> His breathing evens out before mine does.
>
> Annoying.
>
> I stare at the ceiling.
>
> Five minutes ago, wanting him was simple. Wanting has hands. Teeth. Something you can do about it.
>
> This is worse.
>
> His fingers find mine beneath the blanket.
>
> He doesn't say anything.
>
> Neither do I.
>
> I hold on.

Purpose: preserve emotional consequence after sexual payoff instead of sprinting away from it.

## Internal Monologue — System Entanglement

> **[NEW TITLE: OATHBREAKER]**
>
> No.
>
> **Accept title?**
>
> Absolutely not.
>
> **Title automatically equipped.**
>
> Of course it fucking is.
>
> Riven looks at me.
>
> “What?”
>
> “Nothing.”
>
> **OATHBREAKER grants +15% damage against former allies.**
>
> I stop walking.
>
> Former allies.
>
> Not enemies.
>
> Former.
>
> “Actually,” I say, “we have a problem.”

Purpose: the System enters consciousness as lived reality, then changes interpretation and behavior.
