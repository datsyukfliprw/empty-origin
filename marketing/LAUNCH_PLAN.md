This is the working marketing plan for the launch of *Empty Origin*. It should stay synchronized with `book1/PUBLICATION_ROADMAP.md` and `marketing/BRAND_STANDARD.md`.

---

## Launch Objective

Launch *Empty Origin* with a professional, coherent author presence and a reader funnel that can keep working after launch day.

The launch is not built around one viral post. The system is:

**Discovery → Nora Whitcomb profile → book / reader-list landing page → email list → launch / purchase → Book Two readership**

The durable assets are the author brand, email list, retailer pages, reader relationships, reviews, and reusable content library.

# PHASE 2 — Beta / ARC Preparation

**Timing:** During the beta/ARC manuscript stage

The editorial beta/ARC round and the marketing ARC campaign are related but not identical.

## Editorial beta readers

Goal: identify repeated reader-facing problems before production.

Track:

- reader name
- date sent
- completion status
- boredom / skim points
- confusion
- romance satisfaction
- progression satisfaction
- ending satisfaction
- Book Two interest
- actionable repeated issues

Do not ask editorial beta readers to post public reviews unless they independently want to after release.

## Marketing ARC readers

Goal: place finished or near-finished copies with readers likely to review publicly around release.

Before opening ARC applications:

- [ ] Production-ready manuscript or clearly designated ARC edition

- [ ] Final blurb
- [ ] ARC signup form
- [ ] ARC expectations page / email
- [ ] Review-policy language
- [ ] Delivery method selected
- [ ] Tracking sheet ready

### ARC application should collect

- name
- email

- BookTok / Bookstagram / YouTube / blog links, if relevant

# PHASE 4 — Content Engine

## Core content pillars

The Nora Whitcomb feed should not become an endless row of “buy my book” graphics.

Use five recurring pillars:

### 1. The hook

Fast, reader-facing reasons to care.

Examples of angles:

- She arrives in a world where everyone has an origin. Hers is blank.
- Everyone can read her level before they know her name.
- She does not receive a legendary weapon or instant power fantasy.
- Her progression starts with surviving long enough to matter.

### 2. Maya / character

Reader attachment before plot summary.

Angles:

- gamer competence versus real-world vulnerability
- Maya’s humor
- homesickness
- earned confidence
- relationships with Sella and Tolliver
- learning what kind of strength Warder actually means

### 3. World / progression

Show what differentiates the book.

Angles:

- Warder
- origin field
- visible levels
- contracts and social consequences
- transit ruins
- dangerous infrastructure
- dungeon-scale recovery work
- Controlled Release

### 4. Romance / heat

Use accurate promise language without pretending the book is a conventional romance novel.

Angles:

- bisexual romantic tension
- Sella
- Tolliver
- explicit on-page payoff
- messy / nonexclusive emotional structure
- strength, consent, trust, surrender, control

Avoid spoilers that reveal the exact final relationship state before readers have the book.

### 5. Author / behind the book

Make Nora feel like an actual author rather than a book-ad account.

Angles:

- why this genre blend
- favorite worldbuilding problem
- cover evolution
- funny out-of-context lines
- writing / revision discoveries
- gaming influence
- reader questions

# PHASE 6 — Launch Day

Primary message:

**Empty Origin is available now.**

Supporting material can rotate around:

- the one-sentence hook
- strongest reader-fit statement
- cover
- purchase / KU link as applicable
- short personal note from Nora

## Launch-day priorities

1. Send the reader-list email.
2. Publish the primary launch posts.
3. Update profile links from “coming soon” to “read now.”
4. Pin the launch post where useful.
5. Confirm retailer pages are functioning.
6. Respond to genuine reader comments.
7. Re-share reader / ARC posts with permission.
8. Record baseline metrics.

Avoid posting the same sales message every hour.

---

# PHASE 7 — Launch Week

Launch day is the opening bell, not the entire fight.

During the first seven days:

- rotate hook posts
- share spoiler-free reader reactions
- publish one or more character-focused pieces
- publish a progression / worldbuilding piece
- publish romance-reader-fit content
- send one follow-up email if there is a real reason
- thank ARC readers
- continue creator outreach selectively
- monitor retailer / social conversion signals

Do not publicly argue with reviews.

---

# PHASE 8 — Weeks 2–8 After Launch

Shift from “release event” to **reader acquisition**.

Continue:

- strongest hook variants
- excerpts
- memes / relatable character content
- reader reactions
- behind-the-book material

- newsletter growth

Identify which posts actually produce:

- profile visits
- link clicks
- reader-list signups
- saves
- shares
- meaningful comments
- sales / page reads where measurable

Stop treating raw impressions as the primary success metric.

# Guardrails

- Do not market *Empty Origin* as a conventional romance with a guaranteed exclusive HEA.
- Do not hide that the book contains explicit sexual content when speaking directly to ARC readers.
- Do not market it as pure crunchy-stat LitRPG if the actual reading experience is more character / social-system driven.
- Do not promise Earth-return resolution.
- Do not reveal late-book relationship outcomes in generic discovery copy.
- Do not buy fake followers, reviews, or engagement.
- Do not require positive ARC reviews.
- Do not chase every platform equally.
- Do not let marketing work reopen a frozen manuscript without a real reader-facing reason.

## Working Principle

The launch should make it easy for the right reader to answer three questions quickly:

**What is this?**  
Adult progression fantasy with a distinctive social/System hook, dangerous exploration, humor, and complicated desire.

**Why would I care?**  
Maya starts exposed, unreadable, and socially unanchored, then earns her class, competence, relationships, and place in the world.

**What do I do next?**  
Join the reader list now. Read *Empty Origin* when it releases.
