## Brand Language Guidance

Do **not** overload the permanent author bio with a long stack of genre labels such as LitRPG, romantasy, smut, isekai, dungeon crawler, etc.

Those terms are useful for discovery and should be deployed strategically in:
- Posts
- Captions
- Hashtags
- Retailer metadata
- Ads
- Launch copy
- Audience targeting

Keep the permanent Nora Whitcomb author identity cleaner and more durable across future books.
